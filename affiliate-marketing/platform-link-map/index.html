Affiliate basics for Malaysia and Indonesia

Which marketplace links to which social platform in Southeast Asia, and what each link means for you

A map of how Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop connect to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and everything else, with the rules and payout speeds from official pages.

Reka Amorra7 Nov 20267 min read

The short version

  • Native tagging exists on only some routes: Shopee with Instagram, Facebook and YouTube; Lazada with Facebook; TikTok Shop with TikTok. The rest are plain links.
  • Each native link stays with its platform. YouTube says sharing your YouTube affiliate links outside YouTube can suspend your affiliate features. TikTok Shop’s policy bans off-platform links in its content.
  • Payout speed differs a lot. Shopee pays weekly (Indonesia terms). TikTok Shop follows the seller’s settlement, 1 to 15 days after delivery on the Malaysia page, longer if flagged. YouTube pays 60 to 120 days after purchase.
  • Register every account where you post Shopee links. Indonesia’s terms treat unreported accounts as a violation.
  • Some links are not confirmed: Lazada on Instagram, Lazada on YouTube, and Threads, X, Pinterest or WhatsApp as official programmes.

An affiliate in Malaysia or Indonesia can post on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and Threads, and promote Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop. Three marketplaces and five kinds of channel make 15 possible links.

Some of those links are built into the platform. Some are plain links. Some are not allowed. The map below sorts all 15.

How fast each route pays

ShopeeWeekly

Paid every week by bank transfer. Under Rp500,000 goes to ShopeePay (Indonesia terms). I did not find the Malaysia schedule on an official page.

TikTok ShopDays

Paid after the seller’s order settles: 1, 3, 8 or 15 days after delivery on the Malaysia page, and up to 30 days if TikTok flags the account or order.

YouTubeMonths

Paid through AdSense 60 to 120 days after purchase, and reversed if the item is returned.

LazadaNot confirmed

I did not find an official payout schedule. Third-party guides say monthly, which I could not verify.

Three rules that cross platforms

Keep each native link on its own platform

YouTube says sharing your YouTube affiliate links outside YouTube can suspend your affiliate features. TikTok Shop bans links and handles that send viewers off-platform in its content.

One marketplace per piece of content

Shopee Indonesia’s terms say content must not carry links to other e-commerce platforms. Pick one marketplace for each post.

Register every account that carries links

Indonesia’s terms treat links on accounts not reported to Shopee as a violation. Malaysia asks for a public, verified account at sign-up. Facebook must be a Page, not a personal profile.

One Shopee detail: indirect orders

If a buyer clicks your link but buys a different item, that is an indirect order. Shopee’s seller pages say the seller-funded commission on indirect orders is half of the rate the seller set. In Malaysia that is 50% from 24 May 2026, up from 30%. Your dashboard shows the direct and indirect split, and that is the number to trust.

What is still not confirmed

  • Lazada on Instagram: reports say it will follow, and I found no sign that it is live.
  • Lazada on YouTube: nothing found.
  • Threads, X, Pinterest and WhatsApp as official programmes: none found.
  • Whether a Lazada link pasted into Instagram’s Add products works: Instagram says the product must be in a Meta commerce catalog.
  • Shopee Malaysia’s and Lazada’s payout schedules on official pages.

What I would do

The following are my own analysis, not findings from the sources.

Draw your own map

List every platform you post on and the marketplace each link goes to. Mark each as native or plain.

Measure: every platform on your list has one marketplace next to it.

Test a native tag against a plain link

Where a native tag exists, post one piece with the tag and a similar one with a plain link, two weeks each. Compare orders and commission per post.

Measure: commission per post, tag versus plain link.

One marketplace per post

Do not mix links to two marketplaces in one post or video.

Measure: zero posts with links to two marketplaces.

Plan cash by route

Write down the expected payout date for each route. Do not plan YouTube money like Shopee money.

Measure: a calendar with one payout date per route.

Keep an account register

Keep a list of the accounts that carry Shopee links and check that each one is registered in Shopee Affiliate.

Measure: no account carries links without being registered.

Recheck the unknowns monthly

Lazada on Instagram may launch. Check the not-confirmed list once a month and date your note.

Measure: one dated note per month.

Save this: check before you add a new platform

马来西亚和印尼的联盟营销基础

东南亚哪个电商平台能连接哪个社交平台，以及每条连接对你意味着什么

一张地图，看 Shopee、Lazada 和 TikTok Shop 怎样连接 Instagram、Facebook、YouTube、TikTok 和其他平台，附上官方页面里的规则和付款速度。

Reka Amorra2026 年 11 月 7 日阅读约 7 分钟

先看结论

  • 原生标记只存在于部分路线：Shopee 对应 Instagram、Facebook 和 YouTube；Lazada 对应 Facebook；TikTok Shop 对应 TikTok。其余都是普通链接。
  • 每条原生链接都留在自己的平台上。YouTube 说，把 YouTube 联盟链接分享到 YouTube 以外，可能会暂停你的联盟功能。TikTok Shop 的政策禁止在它的内容里放平台外的链接。
  • 付款速度差别很大。Shopee 每周付款（印尼条款）。TikTok Shop 跟着卖家的结算周期，马来西亚页面写签收后 1 至 15 天，被标记则更久。YouTube 在购买后 60 至 120 天付款。
  • 把每个发 Shopee 链接的账号都登记。印尼条款把没有报备的账号当作违规。
  • 有些还没确认：Lazada 在 Instagram、Lazada 在 YouTube，以及 Threads、X、Pinterest 或 WhatsApp 作为官方计划。

马来西亚或印尼的联盟创作者可以在 Instagram、Facebook、YouTube、TikTok 和 Threads 上发布，推广 Shopee、Lazada 和 TikTok Shop。三个电商平台加五类渠道，共有 15 种可能的连接。

有些连接是平台内置的，有些只是普通链接，有些是不允许的。下面的地图把 15 种都分好类。

有官方帮助页面的，我读官方页面；产品发布则看新闻报道。只找到第三方指南的地方，我会说明。

每条路线多快付款

Shopee每周

每周通过银行转账付款。低于 Rp500,000 的转入 ShopeePay（印尼条款）。我没有在官方页面找到马来西亚的付款日程。

TikTok Shop几天

卖家的订单结算后付款：马来西亚页面写签收后 1、3、8 或 15 天，如果 TikTok 标记账号或订单，最多 30 天。

YouTube几个月

购买后 60 至 120 天通过 AdSense 付款，如果商品被退回会被撤销。

Lazada未确认

我没有找到官方的付款日程。第三方指南说是每月，我无法核实。

三条跨平台的规则

让每条原生链接留在自己的平台上

YouTube 说，把 YouTube 联盟链接分享到 YouTube 以外，可能会暂停你的联盟功能。TikTok Shop 禁止在它的内容里放把观众带出平台的链接和账号名。

一条内容只放一个电商平台

Shopee 印尼条款说，内容不能带其他电商平台的链接。每个帖子选一个电商平台。

登记每个带链接的账号

印尼条款把没有向 Shopee 报备的账号上的链接当作违规。马来西亚要求注册时用已验证的公开账号。Facebook 必须是主页，不能是个人档案。

Shopee 的一个细节：间接订单

如果买家点了你的链接却买了别的商品，这就是间接订单。Shopee 的卖家页面说，间接订单中由卖家出资的佣金，是卖家所设比例的一半。在马来西亚，从 2026 年 5 月 24 日起是 50%，之前是 30%。你的后台会显示直接和间接订单的拆分，以这个数字为准。

还没确认的部分

  • Lazada 在 Instagram：报道说之后会跟进，我没有找到它已上线的迹象。
  • Lazada 在 YouTube：什么都没找到。
  • Threads、X、Pinterest 和 WhatsApp 作为官方计划：没找到。
  • 把 Lazada 链接粘贴到 Instagram 的 Add products 能不能用：Instagram 说商品必须在 Meta 的商品目录里。
  • 官方页面上 Shopee 马来西亚和 Lazada 的付款日程。

我会怎么做

以下是我自己的分析，不是来源里的结论。

画出你自己的地图

列出你发布的每个平台，以及每条链接通向哪个电商平台。标明是原生还是普通。

衡量： 清单上的每个平台旁边都有一个电商平台。

拿原生标记和普通链接做对比测试

有原生标记的地方，发一条带标记的内容和一条类似的带普通链接的内容，各两周。比较每条帖子的订单和佣金。

衡量： 每条帖子的佣金，标记对比普通链接。

一个帖子只放一个电商平台

不要在一个帖子或视频里混放两个电商平台的链接。

衡量： 没有帖子同时带两个电商平台的链接。

按路线规划现金

写下每条路线预计的付款日期。不要把 YouTube 的钱当作 Shopee 的钱来规划。

衡量： 每条路线都有一个付款日期的日历。

做一份账号登记表

列出所有带 Shopee 链接的账号，并检查每一个都已在 Shopee 联盟登记。

衡量： 没有未登记的账号带链接。

每月重查一次未确认的部分

Lazada 在 Instagram 可能会上线。每月看一次未确认清单，并给笔记标上日期。

衡量： 每月一条带日期的笔记。

收藏这个：添加新平台之前先检查
