Affiliate basics for Malaysia and Indonesia

Shopee affiliate on Instagram: what you need, how to connect, and what Shopee has not said yet

Shopee and Meta opened this up in Malaysia and Indonesia in July 2026. This is what the official pages say, side by side, and a small test I would run before moving any work over.

Reka Amorra7 Nov 20266 min read

The short version

  • Since early July 2026, eligible creators in Malaysia and Indonesia can link a Shopee affiliate account to Instagram and tag Shopee products in Reels and Feed.
  • You need an account that is professional or public, with 1,000+ followers, age 18 or older, and no integrity violations.
  • The buyer finishes the purchase on Shopee, not inside Instagram. Shopee pays the commission and shows your earnings on its own platform.
  • The commission rate is set by Shopee and shown when you pick a product. The pages I found do not give the rate, the tracking window or the payout timing for Instagram sales.
  • Indonesia’s page says the rollout is still gradual. Meeting the rules does not guarantee you will see Add products yet.

A second platform helps when the first one changes its rules. Shopee and Meta have just given affiliates in Malaysia and Indonesia one more option.

I read Shopee’s help pages for Malaysia, Indonesia and Singapore, Instagram’s own creator blog, and news reports from the launch. I did not take any figure from blog posts that explain it second hand.

Some details are not published. I mark those clearly instead of guessing.

8 marketsMalaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Vietnam, the Philippines and Brazil
1,000+followers needed, on a professional or public account
30 productsmost you can tag in one Reel, on Indonesia’s page
5 million+creators had linked Facebook to Shopee by March 2026, a Shopee figure

What launched in July

Shopee and Meta announced Instagram affiliate partnerships in early July 2026, after running the same idea on Facebook since 2025. Eligible creators in Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Vietnam, the Philippines and Brazil can link a Shopee affiliate account to an Instagram professional account.

They can then tag Shopee products in Reels and Feed posts. A tagged product shows a shopping icon and a label that says it is eligible for commission. One post can carry several products.

Shopee says more than five million creators had linked a Facebook account by March 2026, and about half were new to its affiliate program. That is Shopee’s own figure.

What you need before you start

The Shopee help pages for Malaysia and Indonesia list the same rules. You must be 18 or older, with an Instagram account in Professional mode or public mode, and 1,000 or more followers. You must follow Instagram’s monetization eligibility standards and partner monetization policies. The account must have no history of integrity violations.

You also need to be registered in the Shopee Affiliate Program. Shopee’s cross-linking FAQ says a standard personal account cannot be linked. One FAQ says a professional account is needed, so switch to professional to be safe.

How to connect, step by step

  1. 1
    Check your account typeSwitch to Professional. The pages also allow a public account (not set to private), but one FAQ says Professional is needed to link.
  2. 2
    Register as a Shopee affiliateYou can do this during the connect flow if you do not have an account yet.
  3. 3
    Open the Professional dashboardGo to Monetization, then Affiliate programs, then Shopee. Tap Connect or Continue and allow access.
  4. 4
    Log in to Shopee and linkIn the Shopee app, tap Continue (or Register and Link) to connect Meta and Shopee.
  5. 5
    Tag products when you postOn a Reel or Feed post, tap Add products, choose Shopee, search by name or brand or paste a product link. The estimated commission shows before you confirm.
The buyer completes the purchase on Shopee. Shopee pays the commission, and your earnings show on the Shopee platform.

How the money works, as far as the pages say

What the pages say

  • Commission rates are set by Shopee and shown while you pick a product.
  • Commission is paid by Shopee. Total earnings show on the Shopee platform.
  • Commission is calculated on each validated purchase made through your content.
  • Sales from each linked Instagram account and Facebook page earn commission.

What I did not find

  • The commission rate itself, or how it compares with a normal Shopee affiliate link.
  • How long the tracking window is for Instagram sales.
  • When Instagram commissions are paid.
  • What counts as a “validated” purchase.

Shopee’s pages call it “additional commission”, so I cannot tell whether it sits on top of your normal affiliate commission or replaces it. The product picker shows an estimate, and that is the best check you have.

Shopee and Meta are also testing affiliate ads in a limited rollout in some Southeast Asian markets. With your consent, a seller or brand can boost your affiliate content as a clearly labelled paid promotion. Campaign Indonesia reports that Meta’s ad reports will not show the purchases directly, because purchase data sits in Shopee’s system. It also reports no official word yet on when the test widens.

Why checkout stays on Shopee

In the launch markets, reports say the purchase finishes on Shopee and not inside Instagram. Campaign Indonesia explains the reason in Indonesia: Article 21(3) of Trade Ministry Regulation 31 of 2023 stops social commerce platforms from handling payments on their own systems, and it reports that Meta’s licence in Indonesia only lets Instagram and Facebook show product promotions.

What I would do

The following are my own analysis, not findings from the sources.

Check your dashboard before you plan

Open the Professional dashboard, then Monetization, and see whether Shopee appears under Affiliate programs. If it does not, you know before you spend time on content.

Measure: you can see Shopee listed, yes or no.

If Add products is missing, wait and check weekly

Indonesia’s page says the rollout is gradual, so a missing button does not mean you did something wrong. Keep sharing links the way you do now, and check again each week.

Measure: the date you first see the button.

Run a small test, not a switch

Post 6 Reels over 2 weeks with tagged Shopee products. Note the orders and commission for each Reel on Shopee, and the hours each Reel took you. Compare commission per hour with your other platform.

Measure: commission per hour, Instagram versus your other platform.

Take your numbers from Shopee

Shopee shows your earnings, not Instagram. Write down the estimate you saw in the product picker, then compare it with the final figure Shopee shows.

Measure: estimate versus final, in percent, from Shopee.

Say it is an affiliate link in your caption

The tag carries a commission label, but write it in your own words too: “I earn a commission if you buy through this tag.” It keeps things clear for your audience.

Measure: every affiliate post has the line.

Set your terms before you agree to an ad

If a seller wants to boost your Reel as a paid promotion, it needs your consent. Ask what you get, for how long, and whether you can switch it off. The pages I found do not say.

Measure: terms written down before you say yes.

马来西亚和印尼的联盟营销基础

Instagram 上的 Shopee 联盟营销：需要什么、怎么连接，以及 Shopee 还没说清楚的事

Shopee 和 Meta 在 2026 年 7 月向马来西亚和印尼开放了这项功能。这里是官方页面的说法，两国对照，还有我在转移任何工作之前会先做的一个小测试。

Reka Amorra2026 年 11 月 7 日阅读约 6 分钟

先看结论

  • 从 2026 年 7 月初起，马来西亚和印尼符合条件的创作者可以把 Shopee 联盟账号连接到 Instagram，并在 Reels 和 Feed 里标记 Shopee 商品。
  • 你需要一个专业账号或公开账号，有 1,000 个以上粉丝，年满 18 岁，并且没有诚信违规记录。
  • 买家在 Shopee 完成购买，不是在 Instagram 里面。佣金由 Shopee 支付，你的收入也显示在 Shopee 自己的平台上。
  • 佣金比例由 Shopee 决定，在你选商品时显示。我找到的页面没有写比例、追踪期，也没有写 Instagram 订单的付款时间。
  • 印尼的页面说，推出还是分批进行。符合条件不代表你现在就能看到 Add products。

当第一个平台改规则时，有第二个平台会有帮助。Shopee 和 Meta 刚刚给了马来西亚和印尼的联盟创作者多一个选择。

我看了 Shopee 马来西亚、印尼和新加坡的帮助页面、Instagram 自己的创作者博客，还有发布时的新闻报道。我没有从二手转述的博客里取任何数字。

有些细节没有公布。我会清楚标出来，不去猜。

8 个市场马来西亚、印尼、新加坡、泰国、台湾、越南、菲律宾和巴西
1,000+需要的粉丝数，账号为专业或公开
30 个商品一条 Reel 最多可标记的数量，据印尼页面
500 万+截至 2026 年 3 月把 Facebook 连接到 Shopee 的创作者，Shopee 提供的数字

7 月推出了什么

Shopee 和 Meta 在 2026 年 7 月初宣布了 Instagram 上的联盟合作，同样的做法从 2025 年起已经在 Facebook 上运行。马来西亚、印尼、新加坡、泰国、台湾、越南、菲律宾和巴西符合条件的创作者，可以把 Shopee 联盟账号连接到 Instagram 专业账号。

连接后，他们可以在 Reels 和 Feed 帖子里标记 Shopee 商品。被标记的商品会显示购物图标和“可获佣金”的标签。一个帖子可以带多个商品。

Shopee 表示，截至 2026 年 3 月，已有超过 500 万名创作者连接了 Facebook 账号，其中约一半是第一次加入它的联盟计划。这是 Shopee 自己的数字。

开始前你需要什么

Shopee 马来西亚和印尼的帮助页面列出的条件相同。你必须年满 18 岁，Instagram 账号为专业模式或公开模式，并且有 1,000 个或以上粉丝。你必须遵守 Instagram 的变现资格标准和合作伙伴变现政策。账号不能有诚信违规的记录。

你还需要注册 Shopee 联盟计划。Shopee 的跨平台连接 FAQ 说，普通个人账号不能连接。有一份 FAQ 说需要专业账号，所以为了保险，先切换成专业账号。

怎么连接，一步一步

  1. 1
    检查你的账号类型切换成专业账号。页面也允许公开账号（不设为私密），但有一份 FAQ 说连接需要专业账号。
  2. 2
    注册成 Shopee 联盟会员如果还没有账号，可以在连接过程中注册。
  3. 3
    打开专业后台进入变现（Monetization），再到联盟计划，再选 Shopee。点“连接”或“继续”，并授予访问权限。
  4. 4
    登录 Shopee 并关联在 Shopee 应用里点 Continue（或 Register and Link），把 Meta 和 Shopee 连起来。
  5. 5
    发布时标记商品在 Reel 或 Feed 帖子上点 Add products，选 Shopee，按名称或品牌搜索，或粘贴商品链接。确认之前会显示预估佣金。
买家在 Shopee 完成购买。佣金由 Shopee 支付，你的收入显示在 Shopee 平台上。

钱是怎么算的，只说页面写了的部分

页面写了什么

  • 佣金比例由 Shopee 决定，在你选商品时显示。
  • 佣金由 Shopee 支付。总收入显示在 Shopee 平台上。
  • 佣金按每一笔通过你的内容完成的有效购买计算。
  • 每个已连接的 Instagram 账号和 Facebook 主页带来的销售都能获得佣金。

我没有找到什么

  • 佣金比例本身，或者它和普通 Shopee 联盟链接比起来如何。
  • Instagram 订单的追踪期有多长。
  • Instagram 佣金什么时候付。
  • 什么算“有效”购买。

Shopee 的页面把它叫作“额外佣金”，所以我没法确定它是加在你原来的联盟佣金之上，还是取代它。选商品时会显示预估数字，这是你手上最好的核对方法。

Shopee 和 Meta 还在东南亚部分市场小范围测试联盟广告。经过你的同意，卖家或品牌可以把你的联盟内容作为清楚标注的付费推广来加推。Campaign Indonesia 报道说，Meta 的广告报告不会直接显示购买数据，因为购买数据在 Shopee 的系统里。它还报道说，官方还没有说测试什么时候扩大。

为什么结账仍然在 Shopee

在这次上线的市场，报道说购买是在 Shopee 完成，而不是在 Instagram 里面。Campaign Indonesia 解释了印尼的原因：贸易部长条例 2023 年第 31 号第 21 条第 3 款禁止社交商务平台在自己的系统里处理付款，它还报道说 Meta 在印尼的牌照只允许 Instagram 和 Facebook 展示商品推广。

马来西亚和印尼对照

已经开放了吗？

马来西亚是。马来西亚页面最后更新于 2026 年 10 月 29 日。
印尼是。印尼页面说这仍是早期推出，会逐步向所有符合条件的创作者开放。

谁可以参加

马来西亚年满 18 岁，专业或公开账号，1,000+ 粉丝，没有诚信违规，遵守 Meta 的变现政策。
印尼条件相同。

每个帖子的上限

马来西亚我读到的马来西亚页面没有写上限。它只讲了 Reels 的步骤，不过发布时的报道说 Feed 帖子也可以。
印尼每条 Reel 最多 30 个商品。每个 Feed 轮播最多 20 个商品。

如果看不到 Add products

马来西亚页面没有说原因。Instagram 自己在 2026 年 3 月的文章说，这个工具会分批推到它的 22 个商务市场。
印尼页面说符合条件不代表你现在就能标记商品，因为推出是分批的。

我会怎么做

以下是我自己的分析，不是来源里的结论。

规划之前先看你的后台

打开专业后台，进入变现，看 Shopee 有没有出现在联盟计划下面。如果没有，你在花时间做内容之前就知道了。

衡量： 你看到 Shopee 列在那里，是或否。

如果没有 Add products，先等，每周检查一次

印尼页面说推出是分批的，所以按钮没出现不代表你做错了。照常分享链接，每周再看一次。

衡量： 你第一次看到这个按钮的日期。

先做小测试，不要直接转移

两周内发 6 条带 Shopee 标记商品的 Reel。在 Shopee 上记下每条 Reel 的订单和佣金，还有你做每条 Reel 花的小时数。把每小时佣金和你的另一个平台比较。

衡量： 每小时佣金，Instagram 对比你的另一个平台。

数字从 Shopee 拿

显示你收入的是 Shopee，不是 Instagram。把选商品时看到的预估记下来，再和 Shopee 最后显示的数字比较。

衡量： 预估和最终金额的差距，用百分比，数据来自 Shopee。

在文案里写明这是联盟链接

标记本身带有佣金标签，但你也用自己的话写一句：“如果你通过这个标记购买，我会得到佣金。”这样对观众更清楚。

衡量： 每个联盟帖子都有这句话。

同意加推广告之前先定好你的条件

如果卖家想把你的 Reel 作为付费推广加推，需要你的同意。问清楚你能得到什么、持续多久，以及能不能随时停止。我找到的页面没有写。

衡量： 同意之前，条件已经写下来。

收藏这个：连接和测试之前先检查
